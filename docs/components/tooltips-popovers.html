Tooltips & Popovers

Lightweight tooltips for short hints; structured popovers for title, body, and actions. Popover layout: 7305-789.

Tooltip

Use for brief, non-interactive hints. Position with utilities (absolute, bottom-full, etc.).

Popover

Popovers use .popover with .popover__panel (bordered surface) and popover--placement-{top|bottom|left|right} for caret position. The caret is a shared .popover__caret-svg (rotate per side). Figma: 7310-39799. Include .popover__header (uppercase title + rule), .popover__body, and .popover__footer with .popover__actions. Wire role="dialog" / focus when used as a real overlay.
